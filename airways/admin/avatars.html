Avatars

Four independent flags of every picture. forbidden: shown nowhere, even to the one who has it. common: a passenger gets it at random. pilot: a pilot may choose it for himself. special: for the special journeys. A change is written to the file of the server at once. A picture which a pilot has chosen stays his even when the pilot flag is taken off.
